How to play

JackpotDesk does not sell lottery tickets. Use it to decide whether to play and which slips to take to a store. You must be of legal lottery age in your jurisdiction.

1. Pick the game

Use Powerball or Mega Millions in the header. That switch applies to the whole desk. Changing games clears pool tickets so you do not mix boards.

2. This week

Advertised jackpot and cash value load from a public lottery feed. Tickets sold is an estimate. Edit any field. The board shows implied odds and expected value after tax. Most weeks the honest read is to sit it out.

3. Tickets

Generate random lines. The tool redraws birthday patterns, sequences, and recent official white sets. Hit chance stays the same as Quick Pick. The point is fewer jackpot splits if you win.

4. Map

Public jackpot tickets by state sold, not a prediction and not a store map. Use it to see where big hits have been, not where to play next.

5. Pool

Add members and shares. Mint unique tickets into the board. Mark who paid. After a drawing, settle against the last official numbers and split by paid shares. Copy a share link or print a playslip. Buy the actual tickets at a licensed retailer.

6. Why this

Method and FAQ. Same hit odds. The edge is skipping crowded tickets and skipping drawings that are not worth the stake.
