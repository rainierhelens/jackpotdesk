Lottery Lab

We asked the model

It cannot beat Quick Pick. Powerball 1 in 292,201,338. Mega Millions 1 in 290,472,336. A transformer does not change that.

Language models predict the next word. Neural nets fit patterns in data. A lottery drawing is a mixer. Last week’s balls are not a prompt. There is no serial to complete.

Independent labs have already run the heavy stack (transformers, Bayesian nets, causal tests) on Powerball and Mega Millions history. They found no exploitable signal. JackpotDesk did not retrain those models. We take the result: do not sell a forecast.

What AI picks actually do

Ask a chatbot for “lucky Powerball numbers” and you often get birthdays, 7s, and numbers people just saw on TV. Those still hit as often as any other set. Then they get split. That is a worse ticket, not a smarter one.

What we do instead

Draw uniform random boards. Redraw crowded public patterns. Same hit odds as the retailer Quick Pick. If you win the jackpot, you are less likely to share it. Past results do not predict the next drawing. This is not a lottery courier. Buy at a licensed retailer.

Build an uncrowded slip Free. No account. Same odds as Quick Pick, fewer splits if you win.
