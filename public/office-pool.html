Pool

Run the office pool here

A lottery pool is more tickets plus a ledger. JackpotDesk keeps members, shares, unique slips, and who paid in this browser. It does not collect money, hold tickets, or mail winnings. Someone still walks to a licensed retailer.

Open the pool tab Add names and shares, mint tickets, mark paid, settle after the drawing.

What you track

  • Members and how many shares each person holds
  • Unique random tickets minted into the board (same filters as Tickets)
  • Who has paid for this drawing
  • Settle against the last official numbers and split by paid shares
  • A share link or JSON export if you want a copy off this device

Changing Powerball / Mega Millions in the header clears pool tickets so you do not mix boards. Print a playslip from the desk, then buy the actual tickets in store.

Same odds per dollar

Ten people buying ten unique tickets do not raise anyone’s hit chance per dollar versus buying those tickets alone. The pool only spreads cost and any prize. Hit odds stay the game’s published odds. JackpotDesk does not improve your chance of winning.

Not a courier

JackpotDesk is not a lottery, not affiliated with MUSL or any state lottery, and not an app that buys tickets for you. Pool data stays on your device unless you copy it. Must be of legal lottery age. See responsible gaming and terms.
