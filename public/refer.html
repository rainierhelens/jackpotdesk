What they get

The same desk you use: this week’s line, unique tickets, map, and a pool that lives in their browser. Nothing is synced to a JackpotDesk account because there is not one.

Sharing a pool is different

If you already have a syndicate board, use Copy share link on the Pool tab. That URL carries members and tickets for that pool only. It is not a referral. Whoever opens it loads the board in their own browser.
