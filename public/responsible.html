Responsible gaming

JackpotDesk prices lottery drawings. It does not make them a good bet. Most weeks expected value after tax is negative. Sitting out is the usual honest answer.

You must be of legal lottery age where you live. If play is no longer entertainment — chasing losses, hiding spend, borrowing to buy tickets — stop and get help.

1-800-GAMBLER National problem-gambling helpline (US). 24/7.

Online chat and resources: ncpgambling.org. If you are outside the US, use your local gambling-help service.

How this desk is meant to be used

  • Treat a ticket as a small entertainment spend, not income.
  • Do not raise the stake because last week lost.
  • A “rare plus-EV” sketch is still not an investment.
  • Unique tickets do not hit more often. They only split less if they hit.

Pools

Write the split rule down before anyone pays. Unpaid shares should not collect. JackpotDesk’s board is a reminder, not a contract with the lottery.
