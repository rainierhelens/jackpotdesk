Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

JackpotDesk is a free browser tool at jackpotdesk.com. We do not require an account. Pool names, members, and tickets are stored in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers.

Data we use

  • Official drawing history loaded in your browser from New York State Open Data.
  • Numbers you type (jackpot, tax, pool members). These stay on your device.

Advertising

We may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to serve and measure ads. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses ads data. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Analytics

We do not currently load a first-party analytics product. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may collect standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) as part of operating the service.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository.
