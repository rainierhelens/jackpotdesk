JackpotDesk

About

A free browser tool at jackpotdesk.com.

JackpotDesk is a desk for Powerball and Mega Millions. It does not sell tickets, does not pick “lucky” numbers, and does not raise your chance of hitting. Every legal combination is equal.

What it is for

Two jobs. First, price this week’s drawing using cash value, tax, and split risk so you can skip a bad ticket. Second, mint random tickets that avoid birthdays, sequences, and recent official white-ball sets so a jackpot is less likely to be shared.

An office pool tracker keeps members, shares, paid status, and unique slips in this browser. A map shows public jackpot tickets by the state where they were sold, not a live feed of every $4 winner.

Lottery Lab is the AI-search page: models cannot beat Quick Pick. The desk fades crowded tickets instead of forecasting numbers.

What it is not

Not a lottery. Not affiliated with the Multi-State Lottery Association, Powerball, Mega Millions, or any state lottery. Not financial, tax, or gambling advice. You still buy any ticket at a licensed retailer.

Cost

The site is free. No account. Pool data stays on your device unless you copy a share link or export JSON yourself. Optional tips keep hosting and archives on. They are a gift, not a pass, and they do not raise hit odds.

Write the desk if something is broken or you want more of the ranked field. No name required. The desk does not send winning numbers.
