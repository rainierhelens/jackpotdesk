How to read this

History, not a tip

A high claim count means the Lottery listed more claimed prizes at that address in this window. Busy stores sell more tickets. A fat-ticket shop can show a huge dollar figure from one claim. That is not a luckiest-store crown. Same hit odds at every licensed retailer.
