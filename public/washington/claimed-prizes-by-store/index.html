Washington’s Lottery winners search · last ~365 days

Every licensed retailer has the same chance of selling a jackpot ticket. Location does not change hit odds. This page counts claimed prizes Washington’s Lottery listed in the last year, by the store on that row. Entertainment, not prediction.

The official Winners By Game search publishes claimed prizes, usually $600 and up, with a retailer name, street, and city. This is that list, rolled up by store. It is not every winning ticket sold, not sales volume, and not a reason to drive to a store. Winner names stay on walottery.com.
